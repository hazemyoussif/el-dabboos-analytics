## Margin and receivable quality

All **2,895** confirmed September orders have margin values; each header margin equals its line-margin sum, with **zero exact discrepancies**. Stored percentages match margin/untaxed order value within a 1e-9 float check, with zero mismatches. This percentage check is formula validation, not an exact monetary reconciliation or permission to sum percentages.

Standard Odoo 18 computes order margin from line margin and its percentage using untaxed order value; ordinary line margin uses subtotal less stored line cost × quantity, with a special delivered-quantity case. Stored line cost derives from product cost converted to the line's UOM/currency and may be edited. These semantics are evidenced by the [official order implementation](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/18.0/addons/sale_margin/models/sale_order.py) and [official line implementation](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/18.0/addons/sale_margin/models/sale_order_line.py), plus the installation's metadata and observed header/line agreement. This is order margin, not accounting COGS or accounting gross profit. Source inspection does not prove every possible custom override; a governed margin mapping must retain that distinction.

Current receivables use the **2026-10-06 Baghdad snapshot date** and the exact source observation timestamp in the JSON. There are **66,718** posted customer invoices and **3,502** posted customer refunds in this observation. Every header has matching receivable ledger residual: zero missing ledger rows and zero residual mismatches for both types. The **2,468** positive invoice balances include partially settled invoices; only residual, not original face value, is counted. All sampled invoice header due dates are present. Refund residual totals **-1,047,249.97 IQD** and is reported separately; netting it into outstanding requires business approval. Due today is not overdue. The pure proposed snapshot classifier preserves unknown due dates as unknown rather than assigning zero to both buckets.

These are October observations, not September closing balances. Snapshot inputs and ownership/as-of guarantees are absent from the existing mart, so no September historic AR or receivable publication was fabricated. Opening/journal balances, unallocated payments, credits, instalment due dates and future-dated documents remain explicit business-scope questions before generic outstanding approval. Cash collections were not inferred from residual subtraction.
