### Mapping investigation requested by the user

Do not treat the initial 211 literal department-name matches as overall classification coverage. Read-only investigation of category names and hierarchy found:

- Market tag IDs **268, 322** (`ماركتات`) and **166** (`اسواق`): **1,049 distinct September invoice customers**, counting duplicate same-family tags once.
- ID **177** (`حلويات و معجنات و كرزات`): **189** customers. The source label covers confectionery/bakeries as well as Kirzat; it must retain that meaning.
- Wholesale IDs **173, 174, 175**: **22** customers. Multiple wholesale tags collapse to one candidate family.
- Their union covers **1,258** customers: **1,256** in one candidate family, **two** in multiple families, **670** outside these candidates. Family counts overlap and must not be summed blindly.
- No exact source chain category was identified. Native tag identities and labels can be exposed with explicit unknown/ambiguous buckets; translating them into the requested four official departments remains provisional unless confirmed by source configuration or business definition. Assignment is current-at-extraction, not proof of historical classification.

The two Odoo menus labelled Departments target `hr.department`, not customer classification. `res.partner.business_line_id` is documented as the Sugar / No Sugar business project. `res.partner.is_agent` is documented as salesperson tracking (`هل هو مندوب`) from `visits_and_tracking`; all sampled product supplier candidates have it false. It is not the requested agency.

Global field labels, active view metadata, shared filters and menu actions reveal Brand and Product Category reporting, with many saved invoice analyses grouped by `product_categ_id`. They do not establish an official Agency definition. The sparse product dynamic property is a rack field; the sparse picking property is generic `Property 1`, both configured as character fields with boolean placeholder values in the inspected records. Neither supplies the missing mapping.

Therefore the defensible source candidates are **native customer tags**, **Product Brand**, **Product Category hierarchy**, and **Supplier identity**, each under its own actual meaning. No guessed legal agency links, customer-category priority or silent Brand/Business-Line rename is authorized by this evidence.
