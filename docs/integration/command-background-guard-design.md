# Command background rebase: invariant review

Status: implemented with focused synthetic PostgreSQL verification. Migrations 077–081 and the initialization worker implement private capture, bounded replay, chunked preparation and guarded publication. No company source job or deployment was run.

## Required lifecycle

One registered Command recipe is captured under one live exported source snapshot into private pages. The source seal must check contiguous pages, identities, tenant/source/dataset/cutoff/revision lineage, and independent full-recipe controls before the source owner closes. An unsealed capture is abandoned after owner loss; its pages are never treated as a resumable source snapshot. A sealed capture is source-free input for page-sized canonical replay. Missing recipes remain absent from the mapping and public capabilities until their own exact source evidence is complete.

The canonical partition is created against a trusted parent while its Business member for the month is the exact accepted base partition. The existing `mart.business_delta_origins` row records that parent and base. This row must remain immutable. The candidate's one new recipe is stored using the existing sparse Business origin, replacements, checked roots, source evidence, controls, and per-recipe observations; no accepted Business facts are copied. Existing `mart.business_partition_exact` and `mart.guard_business_member` must prove the complete candidate before its first private serving membership. Preparation then writes one governed chunk per invocation, and the existing prepared binding and seal guards prove each chunk and total coverage. The candidate is invisible to runtime until the trusted pointer advances.

## Parent advance with the same Business base

An ordinary operational publication may advance the trusted pointer while the private Command candidate is preparing. A new private `business` revision can be based on that latest trusted revision if, and only if, **every accepted Business member used by the candidate remains identical** and the target month's accepted Business member is still `origin.base_partition`. Copy the latest non-Business memberships and the candidate Business membership into the new manifest. The original source seal, canonical partition, delta origin, raw facts, controls, and recipe observations do not change.

Migration 065 currently blocks this safe rebase in `mart.guard_business_delta_member`: it requires `next.parent_revision = origin.parent_revision`. Migration 078 retains the immutable origin's original accepted-parent proof and require the *new* revision parent to carry the same exact Business base for the target month. This check runs **before** the existing early return for a partition already used by another revision; otherwise a second private manifest could bypass the rebase condition. No unrelated Business partition may change. The final `mart.guard_trusted_analytics` parent CAS, affected-month/family rules, newer Business cutoff check, `mart.business_partition_exact`, and prepared promotion seal remain in force. `mart.guard_business_delta_origin` does not need to change: the origin is inserted once while its original parent is current and accepted.

Prepared data reuses the existing sealed-binding fast path in migration 074: configuration, input fingerprint and cutoff are recomputed for the new revision before an identical sealed binding can be reused. All Business inputs must remain identical for the metadata-only rebase. The target partition and source evidence are unchanged. New non-Business bindings come from the latest trusted revision and are checked by their existing guards. No amendment to `business_prepared_delta_exact` or copying of physical prepared cells is necessary; its stricter path still applies to newly built sparse data.

If the Business base changed, the sealed capture cannot be rebased as this delta. It must be rejected for this initialization path and a fresh source observation taken against the new accepted base. If source cutoff is no longer newer than the base, it is likewise rejected. This is a base conflict, not permission to retag the capture or declare partial evidence complete.

## Focused proof needed before migration

Synthetic, isolated PostgreSQL cases should cover: same-base operational parent advance during capture and during preparation; reuse of sealed sparse Business data without physical copy; rejection when the target or any other Business member changes; rejection of a forged second membership that previously hit the early-return path; stale cutoff; incomplete source pages and control mismatch; unsealed-owner loss; exact current-month partial capabilities; runtime inability to read any private prepared binding while its Business ancestor remains readable only through a trusted root; and final CAS loss. The owner approved focused tests. Implemented checks cover the same-base rebase, changed-base second-membership rejection, capture privacy and completeness, abandoned unsealed capture, offline replay/resume, completed-only capabilities, private prepared/raw isolation, and atomic publication rollback. Company performance and exhaustive concurrency coverage remain unproven.

Automatic approval review rejected an earlier migration that replaced the origin and member guards without demonstrating SQL and invariant correctness. Its stated concern was persistent weakening of Business parent/lineage enforcement and a privileged publication path. That patch was not applied. The implemented alternative retains the immutable origin guard, tightens the previous second-membership bypass, and reuses already guarded sealed bindings. The focused regression first reproduced the changed-base bypass and then passed with migration 078. The earlier rejected rewrite was not applied.
