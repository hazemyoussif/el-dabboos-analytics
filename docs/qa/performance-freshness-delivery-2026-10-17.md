## User-confirmed serving contract

The expensive work is preparation, not a dashboard-request responsibility. Background refresh builds exact governed snapshots, metric vectors, daily/monthly summaries and indexed detail, validates them, and atomically publishes complete serving coverage. Interactive requests select authorized prepared data for the published revision, period and supported filters, with bounded indexed retrieval and pagination. History reconstruction, large fact scans, aging bucket reconstruction and repeated metric reductions do not belong in the interactive path.

The current scheduling/shared-summary/revision candidate is a partial improvement and does not complete this architecture. In particular, computing a shared CTE over detailed history during a request is not equivalent to preparing a reusable serving dataset in the background. Query tuning and request-local sharing alone do not close the gap.

Prepare and verify all currently exposed metric/grouping/policy/filter/cutoff capabilities before activating a replacement reader. Preserve existing working reports during preparation. The serving registry must identify the prepared grain, policy dimensions, definitions, currency, cutoff and manifest membership that cover each request. A missing prepared capability is a coverage/refresh condition; it must not silently trigger expensive raw-history reconstruction during an interactive request. Detail/evidence reads remain bounded and indexed. Any finer-grain preparation required by a supported request belongs in the background pipeline.

## Freshness implementation gate

Use a governed dataset/dependency registry with a truthful per-family successful source cutoff, definition version, refresh target, job result and active publication. Build the dependency rules from the existing source readers, including payments, reconciliation links, maturity changes, date moves, cancellations/deletions and supported lookup changes. Do not assume invoice/order `write_date` catches every dependency. If a complete prior dependency is unavailable, use a bounded explicit revalidation/sweep of the affected coverage, rather than declaring unaffected months fresh.

A successful no-change validation must retain its own source-cutoff/control evidence and reuse unchanged immutable data members. Do not reinsert every monthly envelope merely because a source lease, batch identifier or extraction timestamp changed. Establish equality over the complete governed fact/customer population, values, dimensions, labels, currency and definitions; totals alone cannot justify reuse. Distinguish last successful source validation from the original member extraction watermark in status and metadata. This is especially important for review/business history: blindly scheduling the current full-month exposure repeatedly would duplicate large immutable fact populations and exhaust storage.

Refreshes retain the existing protected source configuration, read-only source transactions, retained snapshots, independent exact controls, tenant writer lock, immutable members and atomic publication. A failed extraction/control/publication leaves the last trusted serving data active and reports that family stale/failed; a successful operational sync must not mark every family fresh. Closed historical cutoffs may be corrected in a new revision. Current stock/expiry needs an actual as-of observation; no historical inventory is reconstructed from today's values.

A durable new dependency ledger or serving projection requires a concrete migration, expected cardinality/storage, write amplification and rollback evidence for approval before applying it. Do not start full-history reloads, modify source infrastructure or activate additional company jobs as a side effect of performance work. Scheduling runs the healthy deployed image and continues to preflight immutable schema/roles after future deployments.
